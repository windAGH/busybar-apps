# Pick For Me

Cannot decide? A reel picks for you. Choose a list, spin, and the reel slows down in three clicks and lands on one answer in large letters.

## What you see

- The reel: a wide drum that shows the name of the list, with the list's icon beside it.
- A spin: the reel blurs, two options click past, and the third stays. That is the answer, in the largest letters that fit, with a short flash of light.
- Eight lists: Eat, Drink, Wear, Move, Watch, Go, Yes / No, and My list with options of your own.
- Wear answers with a colour and shows a shirt in that colour beside the word.
- Yes / No answers YES or NO with even chances and, once in a while, ASK AGAIN.
- The same answer does not come twice in a row. The only exceptions are Yes / No and lists of three options or fewer, where that would make the answer predictable.

## The lists

- Eat: pizza, sushi, tacos, ramen, burger, salad, curry, pasta, pho, bbq, dumplings, sandwich, noodles, steak.
- Drink: coffee, tea, water, juice, soda, smoothie, boba, beer, wine, cocoa, matcha, lemonade.
- Wear: black, white, navy, grey, red, green, beige, denim, pink, yellow, purple, brown, orange.
- Move: walk, run, yoga, stretch, bike, swim, gym, dance, hike, rest day.
- Watch: comedy, drama, action, sci-fi, horror, doc film, anime, romance, thriller, mystery, rewatch.
- Go: park, cafe, museum, beach, bookshop, cinema, market, library, gallery, stay home.
- Yes / No: yes, no, and rarely ask again.
- My list: whatever you write in the settings.

## Controls

- At every start a short guide shows the controls: five numbered pages, one control on each, the word in large letters under an arrow that points at its control. The top button, a press of the wheel or a turn forward goes to the next page; a turn back or Back goes to the page before (Back on the first page leaves the app). On the last page any key starts the app, and does nothing else. One page says that My list is filled in the settings.
- Turn the wheel: the next or the previous list.
- The top button, or a press of the wheel: spin. Press again for another answer.
- Back: leave the app.

## Settings

- My list: your own options, separated by commas, for example PIZZA, WALK, CALL MUM. Up to 16 options. Capital letters, digits, spaces and the signs plus, minus and exclamation mark are shown. Long options get smaller letters and are cut if they still do not fit. While the setting is empty there is no My list.
- Guide at start: the guide to the controls at every start. On at first.
- Start over: forgets the list you were on and the last answer of every list. My list stays. Switch it on, then off again. This cannot be undone.

## Data and privacy

- Kept on the bar, in the app's own storage: the list you were on and the last answer of each list. Nothing else.
- The app does not use the internet. Nothing leaves the bar. Your own list stays in the bar's settings.
- Every answer is picked by chance on the bar. It is meant for small everyday choices and for fun.
- The built-in lists contain no brand names. This app is not affiliated with any restaurant, drink, film, shop or clothing brand.
