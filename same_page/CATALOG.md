# Same Page

A party game for a group round the desk. A target hides somewhere on a band of light between two opposites, such as COLD and HOT. One player, the clue-giver, peeks at it and says a single clue; the others turn the knob to where they think it is, and Start opens the shutter. The closer the guess, the more points for the whole table.

## What you see

- The main screen, at every start: WORDS, with a pair of opposites at the two ends of the band, or BLANK, with nothing at the ends, for groups that say their own scale aloud. The triangle under Start plays; the press mark with GUIDE at the knob opens the guide.
- The band: a strip of light behind a ribbed shutter.
- The target: a glow under the glass, brightest in its middle. It only shows while the shutter is open.
- The marker: a bright needle that the knob moves.
- The sign under Start says what a press does now: an eye (a peek), arrowheads that meet (shut it), a star (the reveal), arrowheads to the right (the next round).
- Cues: a short line slides over the top row at each step and when nobody does anything: 1 START PEEK, CLUE-GIVER ONLY, 2 GIVE A CLUE, 3 TURN, 4 START REVEAL, START OR OK: NEXT.
- The verdict after a reveal: SPOT ON (4 points), CLOSE (3), JUST (2) or MISSED (0), each sign staged in its own way, with the points of the round and the running total.
- 159 pairs of opposites in three lists, all written for this game, and eight sets of colours.
- The back screen: a prompt for the people on that side. It says in words what is happening and whose move it is, shows where the marker stands, and never shows the target.
- The first time the app is used, a guide of five numbered pages that you turn yourself.

## How a round goes

- The clue-giver sits at the front. Everyone else can sit anywhere: the back screen never shows the target.
- A round is dealt: the shutter shut, a new hidden target, and in WORDS a new pair of opposites.
- The clue-giver presses Start and peeks while the others look away, then presses Start again to shut it.
- The clue-giver says one clue: something that sits on the scale where the target is.
- The others turn the knob to move the marker. From then on Start is the reveal: the shutter opens and the round is scored.
- Start or OK deals the next round.

## Controls

- The app opens on its main screen: turn the knob to choose WORDS or BLANK, Start plays, a press of the knob (OK) opens the guide, Back leaves the app.
- Start (the big button on top): the shutter, open and shut. It is a peek, as often as wanted, until the knob is turned after a peek; then it is the reveal. After a reveal it deals the next round.
- Turn the knob: moves the marker, a column a click, further when it is spun. While a peek is open it shuts the shutter first.
- OK (a press of the knob): a new round, at any time. Before a reveal the round is dropped without points.
- Back: opens a small card with EXIT (leaves the app at once), TOTAL and MENU (the main screen). Turn chooses, Start or OK takes it, Back closes the card.
- The guide: Start, OK or a turn forward for the next page, a turn backward or Back for the page before. Back on the first page or any control on the last closes it, back to the main screen.

## Settings

- Zone: how wide the target is (wide, normal, narrow).
- Words: which pairs are dealt in WORDS (all, everyday, silly).
- Start over: flip this switch to clear everything the app has kept (the total, the order of the pairs, the mode) and see the guide again. Each flip starts over once.

## Data and privacy

- The app keeps one small file on the bar: the order of the pairs, the total, the number of rounds, the mode and whether the guide has been read. A game goes on, with its total, if the app is opened again within three hours.
- Nothing leaves the bar. The app does not use the internet. It makes no sound.
- All words and drawings were made for this app. It is not affiliated with the maker of any other game.
