Baggage Claim is an airport baggage belt for your desk, seen from above: a picture to leave running. Luggage of every kind rides past in an order that never repeats, and now and then something that is not luggage at all.

## What you see
- The hall opens with a roller shutter going up and the lamps striking one by one.
- A belt with moving slats between two steel rims, rubber flaps on the left that every thing has to push through, pools of light it rides through, and the belt's number on the wall.
- 21 kinds of luggage in many colours: hard suitcases, duffels, backpacks, a guitar case, a cello case, golf bags, skis, a surfboard, a cardboard box tied with string, a pet carrier with two eyes in it, a pram, a hat box, a cool box, a steamer trunk, an umbrella and more.
- 21 surprises, each with a small life of its own: a traveller asleep with a hat over the face, a cat on a suitcase, a dog flat out, a goldfish bowl, a rubber duck, a cactus, a robot vacuum driving the wrong way and losing, pizza boxes still steaming, a suitcase that glows when its lid lifts, a toaster, a penguin, a mirror ball, a birthday cake; a snowman in winter, a present in December, a carved pumpkin in October.
- Four belts, 1 to 4, each with its own luggage, wall colour and pace. A belt you come back to has moved on.
- Now and then the belt stops with a jolt, everything lurches, and it starts again.
- The light follows the bar's clock: bright and busy by day, warmer in the evening, dim with long gaps at night, when the sleeping traveller comes by more often.

## Controls
- The app opens on its main screen: the hall already running, with the name on a luggage tag. Start enters at once, pressing the knob (OK) opens the guide, Back exits. Without a key the main screen fades by itself after about four seconds.
- The guide is a short book on the closed shutter: five numbered pages, one control a page. Start, OK or a turn of the knob forward turns the page; a turn back or Back goes a page back. Any key on the last page, or Back on the first, closes it. The first time, it opens by itself.
- Start: a luggage tag names the thing in the middle of the belt in a few dry words. Start again sends it away.
- Turn the knob: the belt's speed - stopped, slow, normal, fast.
- OK, once the main screen has gone: the next belt. The view goes up a floor; after 4 comes 1.
- Back: exit.

## Settings
- Surprises: rare (one thing in eight), often (one in three) or only surprises.
- Light: by the clock, or always day, evening or night.
- Start over: flip this switch to clear everything the app has kept and see the guide again. Each flip starts over once.

## Data and privacy
- The app keeps four small things on the bar: the belt's speed, the belt you were on, that you have seen the guide, and where the Start over switch stood.
- It uses no internet and no account. Nothing leaves the bar.
- Every picture is drawn by the author's own code. The app is not affiliated with any airport, airline or luggage maker, and shows no real brand.
