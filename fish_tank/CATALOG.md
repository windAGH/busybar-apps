# Fish Tank

A small tank of pet fish on your desk. Feed them, but not too often, light the tank as you like it, and open up to three capsules a day in the fish shop to see which fish comes out.

## What you see

- **The tank**: water with shafts of light, sand, plants that sway, and bubbles. Up to twelve fish swim at their own depth and speed.
- **The lamp**: five steps, from a dim evening tank to full light.
- **Feeding**: pellets sink from under the Start button, and the fish come for them.
- **The fish shop**: a capsule sinks in, rocks and opens in a bloom of light. There are twelve kinds of fish: six common (guppy, goldfish, tetra, molly, danio, platy), four rare (angelfish, betta, puffer, blue tang) and two epic (seahorse, golden koi). The chances are about 70, 25 and 5 in 100.
- **Status**: how many fish there are, when they were last fed, the tank's age in days and how full the fish are.
- **After time away**: when you open the app again, a card first says what happened.

## How the fish live

- A fish goes from full to starving in 48 hours. A feeding fills every fish by a quarter.
- Fish that are full should not be fed. The first extra feeding of a day is only a warning; each further one kills a fish.
- A fish that has had nothing for too long starves: the hungriest first, then one more every 12 hours.
- Eight fish are comfortable. With more, one dies every midnight. Twelve is the most.
- Three capsules a day, new ones at midnight. A fish you release still uses its capsule. An empty tank always gets one more capsule.
- All of this goes on while the app is closed.

## Controls

- **Guide**: the first time, five pages show the controls. Start, OK or a forward turn of the knob goes to the next page; a backward turn or Back goes to the page before. On the last page any control starts the app. It does not come again by itself.
- **Tank**: Start feeds. Turn the knob for the lamp, brighter or darker. OK opens the menu. Back leaves the app.
- **Menu**: two pages side by side, the fish shop and the status; turn the knob to go from one to the other. Back returns to the tank.
- **Fish shop**: OK or Start opens a capsule.
- **Questions** (TANK IS FULL, and KEEP or RELEASE): turn the knob to choose, OK or Start to confirm. Back returns to the shop from the first question, and keeps the fish at the second.

## Settings

- **Bubbles**: bubbles rise from the sand now and then.
- **Start over**: flip this switch to clear everything the app has kept: a new tank with two fish, and the guide again. Each flip starts over once.

## Data and privacy

- The app keeps one small file on the bar: the fish, when they were fed, the capsules used today, the day the tank was started, the lamp's step and whether the guide has been seen.
- It does not use the internet. Nothing leaves the bar.
- The fish are ordinary aquarium species drawn for this app. It is not affiliated with any game, film or brand.
