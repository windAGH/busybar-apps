# Chop Chop

A small game of skill: cut the food into pieces of the same length before the time runs out. Turn the knob to place the knife and press Start or OK to cut; the more even the pieces, the higher the score, and one bad cut ends the game.

## What you see

- **The main screen**: a loaf on its board and the name. Every start arrives here. A small triangle under Start moves gently towards the button: Start plays. At the knob stands GUIDE with the mark for a press, a triangle pointing down onto a line.
- **The guide**: five short pages that you turn yourself, one control a page, each with a mark under the control it means and the word for what it does. It opens by itself the first time, and whenever you press the knob on the main screen.
- **Before each round**, a card says how many cuts: 1 CUT, 3 CUTS, up to 6.
- **A round**: the food lies on the board, the knife hovers over it and its shadow shows where it will cut. The line along the bottom is the time: it shrinks from both ends towards the middle, green, then amber, then red in the last second.
- **Ten foods**: a loaf, a baguette, a cucumber, an aubergine, a fish, a carrot, a sausage, a roll cake, tofu and a sushi roll, each with its own inside where it is cut.
- **The judging**: white marks show where the cuts belonged and a coloured line shows each piece (green is right, orange is one column off, red is more). Then the percentage counts up with a verdict: PERFECT, CLEAN, DECENT or ROUGH.
- **Game over**: when a round falls below the pass mark the word is RUINED; you see your total, the rounds you got through and your best. PLAY stands beside Start's triangle and TOP 5 beside the press mark at the knob.
- **Top 5**: your five best games with score, round and date; the newest is in amber.

## How it is scored

- All pieces must have the same length, measured between the outer ends of the food, whatever its shape (a fish counts from nose to tail).
- A round scores 100 minus the average error of the pieces in per cent. A perfect cut is always possible.
- The pass mark starts at 60 and rises to 75. Later rounds have more cuts (up to six) and less time, and from round 8 the food may lie off the middle.
- Cuts you did not make when the time is up count against you.

## Controls

- **Main screen**: the app opens here. Start plays, pressing the knob (OK) opens the guide, a turn of the knob opens the top 5, Back exits.
- **Guide**: Start, OK or a turn of the knob forward goes to the next page; a turn back or Back goes to the page before. Back on the first page, or any key or turn on the last, closes the guide and returns to the main screen.
- **Round**: turn the knob to move the knife, one column a click and faster when you spin it; Start or OK cuts; Back pauses.
- **Pause**: Start resumes, Back quits to the main screen.
- **Game over**: Start plays again, OK opens the top 5, Back goes to the main screen.
- **Top 5**: turn the knob to scroll, Start plays, Back goes back.

## Settings

- **Start over**: flip this switch to clear the five best games and everything else the app has kept, and to see the guide again. Each flip starts over once. This cannot be undone.

## Data and privacy

- The app keeps one small file on the bar: your five best games, and that the guide and the rule card have been shown.
- It uses no internet connection and sends nothing anywhere. Nothing leaves the bar.
- At every start it shows the author's mark for a second and a half.
- All drawings are the author's own. The app is not affiliated with any other game, brand or product of a similar name.
