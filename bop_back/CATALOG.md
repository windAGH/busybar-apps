# Bop Back

A small rhythm game of watch, then play it back. A little act on a stage plays a short phrase, a count runs three, two, one in time with the beat, and then the same bars pass again and Start is your hit.

## What you see

- **Five acts**, each with its own hit and its own mishap: a cat at a drum, a bear at an anvil, a frog and a firefly, a woodpecker at a trunk, a popcorn pan. A new act comes on every three rounds; on the title page you turn the knob to pick the one a game starts with.
- **A round always says what is going on**, in large letters beside the act: WATCH while the act plays the phrase, then one bar of count - 3, 2, 1, and NOW YOU: START under an arrow that points at the Start button - and on the very next beat it is your turn.
- **The strip** along the foot of the screen is the phrase written down: a dot wherever a hit belongs. A lamp walks along it in time. The act strikes when the lamp is on a dot, and in your turn so do you. From level 4 on the dots grow fainter in your turn.
- **The verdict**: SUPERB, OK or TRY AGAIN, with one dot for every hit on its tick. Left of the tick was early, right was late; green is perfect, yellow is a hit, a red ring was not played, a cross is a press out of place.
- **Three hearts**. A round passes with about seven hits in ten, and one press out of place costs nothing. A failed round costs a heart and is played again; every fifth round passed gives a heart back. With no heart left the game is over, and you see your score and your best.
- **Eight levels**: slow, even beats first (90 a minute), then rests, off-beats, quick pairs, two-bar phrases and faster tempos up to 130.

## Controls

- **Main screen**: the app opens on its title. Start plays; turn the knob to choose the act; press the knob (OK) to open the guide; Back leaves the app.
- **The guide**: five short pages show what each control does. It opens by itself the first time, and later whenever you press the knob on the main screen. You turn the pages yourself: Start, OK or a turn of the knob forward goes on, a turn backward or Back goes one page back. A key on the last page, or Back on the first, closes it and you are on the main screen again.
- **In a round**: Start is the hit. Back pauses.
- **Paused**: Start or OK goes on (the round starts again); Back quits to the title.
- **Verdict**: Start goes on at once.
- **Game over**: Start or OK plays again; Back goes to the title.

## Settings

- **Sound**: a drum for every hit the act plays, a click for the count, a ding when it is your turn, a ping for your own hits.
- **Timing**: Relaxed (a hit counts within about a tenth of a second of its beat) or Strict (half of that).
- **Start over**: flip this switch to clear everything the app has kept; the guide then opens by itself once more. Each flip starts over once. It cannot be undone.

## Good to know

- Nothing is drawn for the first time on a beat. The game measures how long the bar takes to answer and sends each picture that much earlier, so the strike you see sits on the beat.
- It also follows, slowly, your own habit of pressing a little early or a little late, so the dots of the verdict match what you felt.

## Data and privacy

- Kept on the bar, in the app's own storage: your best game, the number of rounds you have passed, the act you chose last, your own pressing offset and that you have seen the five pages.
- No internet and no account. Nothing leaves the bar.
- The acts, pictures and sounds were all made for this app. It is not affiliated with any other game or its makers.
