# Hoops

NBA game night on your BUSY Bar. One game at a time as a score strip: both teams, the score, the quarter and the clock, the timeouts each side has left, and along the bottom a line that runs out with the quarter.

## What you see

- **During a game**: the two scores, the quarter over the game clock, seven dots above each score for the timeouts left, and the bottom line: the quarter's time in the two teams' colours, shrinking towards the middle. Now and then the middle shows a run that is on (NYK over 9-0) or the quarter's team fouls (yellow from five).
- **Big play signs** for the game on show: 3 POINTER, followed by the shooter's number and name when the feed gives them; a run of eight points and more; a change of lead; OVERTIME; FINAL.
- **Timeout**: T/O takes turns with the quarter, and the dot of the timeout just called blinks. **Halftime**: Half over a countdown to the second half, an estimate: the feed only says that the break is on.
- **The hour before tip-off**: the game already takes its place, with 0 - 0 and a countdown to the tip-off.
- **Earlier**: the tip-off time and the day in your bar's time zone. **Afterwards**: Final and the score, the loser dimmed.
- **When the season is over**: for 30 days after the last game of the Finals, a champions' banner (gold rules, a cup, the winner's mark and name) takes turns with the result of the Finals in games won. After that the card reads SEASON IS OVER / OFFSEASON, with the days to the next tip-off once the schedule is known.
- Each team is a small jersey in its two colours over its three letters. The app carries no league or team logos.

While a game is on, the app stays on one game: your team's if it is on, otherwise the first one. When no game is on, today's finished games and the ones to come take turns, six seconds each.

## Controls

- **Wheel**: the next or the previous game that is on.
- **OK** (press the wheel): the teams as jerseys over their letters, or as their three letters alone in tall capitals.
- **Start**: a look around the league: the other games that are on, the finished ones, the ones to come, and back to your game. Start again goes back at once, behind a BACK TO LIVE card.
- **Back**: exit.

## Settings

- **My team**: one of the 30 teams. The app starts on its game.
- **Show**: all games, or my team only on the days it plays.
- **Big play signs**: on or off.

A change applies within 10 seconds; no restart is needed.

## Data and privacy

- Scores come from ESPN's public scoreboard endpoints (site.api.espn.com, site.web.api.espn.com and sports.core.api.espn.com), read by the bar itself over Wi-Fi. They are unofficial, need no key and no account, and can change or stop without notice. Live numbers run some 10 to 30 seconds behind the arena.
- The app looks at the game on show every 5 seconds. Between two looks the game clock counts down by itself, never past the value last received, so it can run a few seconds late. A sign is never made up, but one can be missing.
- The app sends no personal data: only plain requests for a day's games, one game at a time, its timeouts and fouls and, after the season, the last game of the Finals and the date the next season starts. On the bar it keeps two small files: the list of the day's games and a count of failed requests.
- This app is not affiliated with or endorsed by the NBA, its teams or ESPN. Team names and abbreviations are used only to say who is playing.

## Without a connection

- The last numbers stay on screen, and the app asks again after 10, 20, 40 seconds and so on, up to every 2 minutes. A live game whose numbers are more than two and a half minutes old shows a grey quarter and --:-- for the clock.
- If nothing could be loaded at all, a card says why: NO WI-FI when the bar is offline, NO DATA / RETRYING when it is online and the hosts do not answer.
- When there is no game today or in the next four days during the season, the card reads NO GAMES / THIS WEEK.
