# Lucky Reels

A three-reel slot machine for your desk. The coins are play coins only: nothing can be bought, won or cashed out, and no real money is involved anywhere.

## What you see

- The machine: three reels with the pay line across the middle, your coins at the left, your bet at the right.
- A spin: the reels blur, then stop one after another from left to right, each with a small clunk.
- A win: the reels that won light up, coins fly into your balance and the number counts up.
- Jackpot: three sevens or three diamonds bring up the JACKPOT sign over the whole screen.
- Out of coins: a short scene with an empty coin bag, then NEW GAME? PRESS OK, and you have 100 coins again.

## What pays

- Three of a kind pay this many times the bet: cherry 5, lemon 10, clover 15, bell 25, diamond 50, seven 100.
- Two cherries anywhere on the line pay 2 times the bet.
- A little more than one spin in four wins something. In the long run the machine gives back about 94 of every 100 coins played. The odds are the same for every bet.

## Controls

- The controls by their names: Start is the big button on top, OK is a press of the knob, Turn is turning the knob, Back is the Back button.
- The app opens on its main screen. Start enters the game. OK opens the guide. Back leaves the app.
- The guide: five numbered pages, one control on each, with what it does in large letters under an arrow that points at it. Start, OK or a turn forward goes to the next page; a turn back or Back goes to the page before. Any key on the last page, or Back on the first, closes it and leads back to the main screen. The first time you open the app the guide opens by itself.
- The machine: Start spins. Turn the knob to set the bet: 1, 2, 5, 10 or 25 coins, never more than you have. OK opens the info pages. Back leaves the app.
- While a win is still counting: Start begins the next spin at once.
- Info pages: your coins and your best win, the pay table, the number of spins. Turn the knob to go through them. OK, Start or Back returns to the machine.
- New game page: OK or Start gives a new game with 100 coins. Back leaves the app.

## Settings

- Quick spins: the reels stop sooner. Off at first.
- Start over: flip this switch to clear everything the app has kept and see the guide again: a new game with 100 coins. Each flip starts over once. This cannot be undone.

## Data and privacy

- Kept on the bar, in the app's own storage: your coins, your bet, your best win and the number of spins. Nothing else.
- The app does not use the internet. Nothing leaves the bar.
- Play coins only. There are no purchases, no prizes and no way to turn coins into anything. It is a game for fun, not gambling for money.
- Every spin is decided by chance on the bar.
- This app is not affiliated with any casino, game maker or slot machine brand.
